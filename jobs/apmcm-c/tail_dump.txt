IX. Conclusions
@@@ p0025-b0008 | {"role": "list_item"}
• The three call types differ in structure. A rule based on the second fundamental and on pulses classifies 94.4% of the calls, while the continuous measures barely beat the baseline; only the peak frequency differs significantly after correction.
@@@ p0025-b0009 | {"role": "list_item"}
• Two-voice calls carry farthest. The effective distances of flat, mod and tv calls are 155, 225 and 245 m for possible recognition, 83, 144 and 177 m for recognition and 41, 85 and 107 m for reliable recognition.
@@@ p0025-b0010 | {"role": "list_item"}
• Calls elicit a response in 64.7% of the experiments against 12.9% for the control. Responding depends on context and group size; latency grows by a factor of 1.33 per 50 m; about one bird responds per playback.
@@@ p0025-b0011 | {"role": "list_item"}
