Supplementary 3: Modelling analyses on playback experiments 
 
Model on Heads up duration 
Model selection 
Priors tested and compared: normal(0,1), normal(0,1.5), normal(0,2), student_t(1,0,1), student_t(1,0,2), 
student_t(3,0,1), student_t(3,0,2), student_t(5,0,1), student_t(5,02).  
 
Results were very robust to the prior (Table 1). Factors were considered to be having a significant effect 
when the confidence interval (CI) of posterior distribution did not cross zero (+ significant positive effect, - 
significant negative effect). A tendency was considered when CI was within the range [-0.05, X>0] or 
[X<0.05] (brackets in the table).
